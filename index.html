Suddendeath!

LuckyDrop 2.0 is a free lucky draw your whole room watches together. Guests scan a QR code, their emoji drops through a Plinko board on the big screen, and rounds knock people out until one is left.

Get LuckyDrop Free and open source

How it works

  1. 1

    Scan

    Guests scan the QR code on the big screen, type a name and pick an emoji. No app and no sign-up.

  2. 2

    Drop

    You tap Start Game on your phone and every ball falls at once. It's real physics, so every drop plays out differently.

  3. 3

    Win

    Land in WIN to stay in. Miss and you explode. The WIN slot narrows each round until one person takes the prize.

First one wins

Final rounds can keep ending with nobody in WIN. Tick one box and the replays stop: misses drop straight back in from the top, and the first ball into WIN takes it. Everyone else ranks by how far down the board they got.

Two themes

Neon for any night. Halloween for a graveyard board, jack-o'-lantern pegs, bats, ghosts, lightning and its own soundtrack. Switch whenever you like from your phone.

Get LuckyDrop

Free under the MIT license. It runs on your own computer or server, and you control it from your phone.

cd luckydrop
npm install
npm run dev
